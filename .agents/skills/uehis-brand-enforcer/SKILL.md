# UEHIS Brand Enforcer Skill

This skill MUST be active whenever designing, implementing, or auditing frontend code for the UEHIS application.

## 1. Anti-Vibe Design Rules (STRICT PROHIBITIONS)
Do NOT implement or suggest any of the following:
- No purple-blue gradients
- No gradient hero text
- No Inter, Geist, or Space Grotesk typography
- No generic "shadcn" appearance
- No glassmorphism
- No bento grids
- No fake testimonials
- No generic SaaS cards
- No rainbow maps
- No AI-generated buzzwords
- No decorative animation overload
- No excessive rounded cards
- No giant shadows
- No fake statistics

## 3. Implementation Guidelines
- The design must be highly utilitarian, scientific, and authoritative.
- Focus on typography legibility and data density.
- Do not add visual flair just for the sake of it.
- All buttons, borders, and surfaces must reflect a serious, institutional tone rather than a modern startup aesthetic.
