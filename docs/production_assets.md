## Key Methodology Constants

- **CRS**: EPSG:32643 (UTM Zone 43N)
- **Grid**: 250 m fixed-origin PN grid
- **U-Net**: 3-model ensemble, 8-way TTA, 96-pixel context, Bartlett weighting
- **Scoring**: Entropy weighting with variance-floor safeguard
- **Carbon**: ETH GlobalCanopyHeight 2020, urban_generic allometry
- **Population**: WorldPop constrained 2020
- **PM2.5**: ACAG V6.GL.02
- **LST**: Landsat 8 Collection 2 Level 2
